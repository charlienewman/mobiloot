## Long description

**Devnet preview. Mobiloot runs on Solana devnet: test SOL has no monetary value, and the eSIM you receive is a demo that does not connect to a network.**

Mobiloot is a mobile plan paid for by your staking rewards. Stake SOL once, keep your principal, and let what it earns pay your phone bill.

How it works:
• Stake SOL from your own wallet. You receive lootSOL, one for every SOL you put in, and it stays in your wallet.
• The pool's staking rewards go towards your plan. One on-chain transaction activates your month, and the app shows how much of the plan your stake covers.
• Install the eSIM with one tap, straight from the app.

What you can do in this preview:
• Sign in with your wallet. No email, no password.
• Stake and withdraw devnet SOL. Withdrawals are 1:1 with no fee.
• Activate the plan and receive a demo eSIM.
• See your coverage, your plan and your orders, and delete your account and data at any time.

Real UK lines are available to a small group of invited pilot wallets. To ask for one, email support@mobiloot.com with your wallet address.

To try it, switch your wallet to devnet and get free test SOL from faucet.solana.com.

Mobiloot never holds your SOL. It is open source under the MIT licence: github.com/charlienewman/mobiloot

Mobiloot was a prize winner at Clock In, the 2026 Solana Mobile hackathon.
