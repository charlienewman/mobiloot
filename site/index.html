How it works

Three taps between your wallet and a working number.

01

Stake SOL from your own wallet.

You receive lootSOL, one for every SOL you put in. It sits in your wallet, not ours, and you can turn it back into SOL at any time.

02

Your rewards pay for the plan.

The pool sends its staking rewards to the plan instead of to you. A single on-chain transaction activates your month, and the app shows how much of the plan your stake covers.

03

Install the eSIM. You're connected.

One tap opens Android's eSIM installer with your code filled in. No shop, no plastic SIM, no waiting for the post.

How it's built

Your keys. Your SOL. Our problem to keep you online.

Self-custodial from the first tap.

Mobiloot never holds your SOL. It goes into a standard Solana stake pool, and the lootSOL you get back lives in your own wallet. Only you can sign it away.

Questions

The ones worth asking.

Is this live with real money?

Not yet. The public app is a preview on Solana's devnet, a test network whose SOL has no monetary value. Everyone can try the full flow with a demo eSIM. Real UK lines are limited to invited pilot wallets while we prepare the mainnet launch.

Where does my SOL go, and can I get it back?

It goes into an SPL stake pool, the standard Solana program for pooled staking, and you receive lootSOL in your own wallet in return. Mobiloot cannot move or spend it. You can withdraw at any time, one SOL for each lootSOL, with no withdrawal fee from Mobiloot.

What happens to my line if I withdraw?

The plan is paid by your stake's rewards, so if your stake drops below what the plan needs, the line is suspended at the next check. Stake again and it resumes.

What if my rewards don't cover the full £10?

Then your stake covers part of the plan and you pay the rest. That top-up is planned for the mainnet pilot. In the preview, the plan unlocks at 0.5 SOL so anyone can try it.

Which phones does it work on?

Mobiloot is built for the Solana Seeker. It also runs on other Android phones that support eSIM and have a wallet that speaks Mobile Wallet Adapter. The mobile plan itself is UK only for now.

Who runs the mobile network?

Lines are provided by Shaka, a UK mobile virtual network operator, through its wholesale API. Mobiloot is an independent project by Charlie Newman, who is a minority shareholder in Shaka. Mobiloot is not run or funded by Shaka.

What are the risks?

Staking is not risk free. The price of SOL can fall, staking rewards vary, and smart contracts can have faults. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Only stake what you are comfortable leaving staked.

What data do you keep, and can I delete it?

Your wallet address, your sign-in sessions and the details of your eSIM order. No name, no email, no card. Email support@mobiloot.com to have it deleted. The privacy policy has the detail.
