3. What Mobiloot is

Mobiloot lets you deposit SOL into a stake pool on Solana and receive lootSOL, a token that represents your share of the pool. The pool is set up so that its staking rewards are paid to Mobiloot's treasury rather than to holders. In return, a wallet that holds enough lootSOL can activate a mobile plan for each billing period, delivered as an eSIM.
